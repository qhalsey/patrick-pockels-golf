About

A coach who's lived inside the game.

Patrick Pockels has been playing competitive golf for over 20 years and teaching for the last five. He's spent that time figuring out what actually moves the needle for amateur golfers — and what's just noise.

He's competed at the highest amateur and pro-am levels, including the Valero PGA Tournament and the 59th Southern California Open Championship in 2022. That tournament experience translates directly into the way he coaches: no fluff, no swing-of-the-month theories — just what holds up under pressure.

Lessons are built around your game, your tendencies, and your goals. Whether you're trying to break 100 or break par, the path is the same: clear feedback, repeatable mechanics, and on-course strategy that actually scores.
